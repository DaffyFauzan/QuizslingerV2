        How to play

        Everyone gets the same equation and six bullets. Each bullet is an answer; only one is right.

        1. Solve the equation in your head.
        2. Tap a bullet to load it. You choose; nothing loads for you.
        3. Aim at an opponent: hold the left mouse button (or your finger) and drag to look around. Tap an outlaw to snap to them. The crosshair turns red when locked on.
        4. Press Fire.
        5. The right bullet hits and ends the round. A wrong bullet is a blank: it is spent and you reload for a moment.

        Six bullets refill every round. Each hit costs a life (3 lives with up to 4 players, 2 with up to 8, 1 with up to 16). Last gunslinger standing wins.
